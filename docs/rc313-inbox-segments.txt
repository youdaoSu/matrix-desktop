Changed two desktop modules: inbox storage codec and store. Large immutable
message/person records are grouped into 256-record gzip members. Unchanged
members reuse compressed bytes, while changed records rebuild only their block.
Each store retains at most 8 MiB of estimated compressed-buffer/reference/key
cache cost; candidate and old cache can coexist transiently during a transaction.
This is not a bound on the complete application or archive memory footprint.
Map copy/equality work yields between batches; decoded 64 MiB safety limit,
capacity, scope, original OS encryption and atomic fsync/rename remain unchanged.
Cache publication follows successful rename. Generic synchronous commits clear
the cache; records use the existing internal immutable replacement contract.

Existing YXINBOX-GZIP-1 envelope and exact JSON remain readable by the prior
decoder. Concatenated gzip members are documented by Node.js since 5.9.0:
https://nodejs.org/download/release/v18.18.2/docs/api/zlib.html#class-zlibgunzip
Actual Electron 33.4.11 native safeStorage reopen also verified the format.
